# Rwanda privacy and regulatory review — sign-off checklist

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice or a compliance certification. Obtain review from qualified Rwanda counsel and the relevant regulator/stakeholders before processing live personal data.

- [ ] Identify the data controller(s), processor(s), sub-processors and written responsibilities.
- [ ] Map every personal-data field, purpose, source, recipient, storage location and retention period.
- [ ] Determine lawful basis and provide clear Kinyarwanda, English and French notices.
- [ ] Complete a privacy impact assessment for location, health-facility readiness, emergency reports and vulnerable populations.
- [ ] Minimize health data; do not put patient names, diagnoses or treatment details into generic sync payloads.
- [ ] Define access controls, least privilege, audit access, export controls and periodic access reviews.
- [ ] Establish correction, access, deletion/retention and complaint handling procedures.
- [ ] Assess cross-border transfers, cloud regions, edge backups and processor contracts.
- [ ] Define encryption/key custody, breach detection, notification and incident response.
- [ ] Review applicable Rwanda data protection and cybersecurity requirements with counsel and the competent authority.
- [ ] Obtain written approval from participating local authorities/community leadership and safeguarding stakeholders.
- [ ] Record counsel/reviewer, version, date, decisions, conditions and evidence links.

Release gate: no live personal or health data until all applicable items are signed off and operational controls are evidenced.
